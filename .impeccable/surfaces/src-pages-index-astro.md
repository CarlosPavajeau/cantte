# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: businesses in Colombia/LatAm choosing a software firm. Action: write by email or WhatsApp. Proof: Cetus and Wappiz, the firm's own products, and their real release history. Constraints: no invented clients, numbers or testimonials; Spanish (Colombia); SEO for "desarrollo de software a la medida" intent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The firm introduces itself through a dated record of what it has shipped, with the visitor's project as the next open entry. Refuses the category default of a slogan hero over three equal service cards and a stock "why us" grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cetus ledger: achromatic ink on paper (oklch chroma 0), Inter Variable tightened at display sizes, Geist Mono only for dates, versions, code and copyable values. 1px rules and 10% ink rings, 6px/8px radii, no shadows except floating surfaces. Color only marks state (emerald "en producción" dot, sky for in-flight). Light and dark themes.

STORY: Visitor sees a firm that ships real products, understands the four services in concrete deliverables, sees Cetus and Wappiz working (API call → DIAN result; WhatsApp booking), reads how a project runs, gets FAQ answers, writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7 columns: h1 at display size, lead, primary "Cuéntanos tu proyecto" (names the action; "Escríbenos" stays in the header) + outline WhatsApp. Right 5 columns: ruled "Registro de entregas" log, mono dates, entries for Wappiz and Cetus milestones, last row an open "Tu proyecto" entry that links to contact.

FORM: Delivery log, position 6 on the ordered list, seed:07fa12df5d3ca669. Signature interaction: the log's open "Tu proyecto" row has a blinking caret; choosing a service elsewhere on the page writes it into that row and into the prefilled email/WhatsApp message.
